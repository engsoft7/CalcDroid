CalcDroid Privacy Policy

App: CalcDroid (io.github.engsoft7.calcdroid)

CalcDroid is a free Android calculator developed and published by Engsoft7. This policy explains how the app handles information during use.

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Summary

The CalcDroid app does not collect, store, or share personal data. It operates on your device, does not request Android permissions, and does not access the internet. All calculations are performed locally.

1. Data on Your Device

Expressions, graphs, and matrices you enter exist only in the app's memory while it is open, so the interface can function, for example, when you rotate the screen.

The app does not save a history or files, and this content does not leave your device.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

CalcDroid does not collect, store, or share personal data. The app does not request Android permissions and does not access the internet. It does not transmit data to its own servers or third parties during operation.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or other third-party services. Installation and distribution are provided through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy ↗

4. Children

The app does not collect children's personal data.

5. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated to explain the changes and show a new update date.
