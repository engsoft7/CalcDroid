CalcDroid Privacy Policy

App: CalcDroid (io.github.engsoft7.calcdroid)

CalcDroid is a free Android calculator developed and published by Engsoft7. This policy explains how the app handles information during use.

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Summary

According to the behavior described for this version, CalcDroid runs locally on your device and does not collect, store, or share personal data. Please verify these statements against the source code and published version before keeping this policy live.

1. Data and On-Device Processing

Expressions, graphs, and matrices entered in the app are kept in the app's memory while it is open so the interface can function. According to the behavior described for this version, the app does not save history or files, and this content does not leave your device.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

According to the behavior described for this version, CalcDroid does not request Android permissions and does not access the internet. The app therefore does not collect or transmit personal data to its own servers or to third parties while it operates.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or other third-party services, as described for this version. Installation and distribution are provided through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy ↗

4. Children

According to the behavior described in this policy, the app does not collect children's personal data.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change, if external libraries or services are added, or to reflect legal requirements. The update date will be changed whenever the policy is revised.

6. Contact
